Anatomy

Every part of it is doing something

Six details that decide how a bra feels by 6pm.

How it holds you

Support is a system, not a wire

01
The band carries the weight

80% of the support comes from the underband, not the straps. It's cut wide and bonded flat so it can pull firm without digging — which is why it doesn't ride up when you lift your arms.

02
The wire frames, it doesn't grip

A flexible wire follows the root of the breast and stops short at the side, clear of the arm crease. You feel an outline, not a pinch.

03
The straps only fine-tune

Soft jacquard elastic with V-slides at the back, so they hold their setting instead of creeping down over the day. Loosen them and the shape stays.
